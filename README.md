# Our Game Shelf

Live at https://ourgameshelf.com

Combines the owned BoardGameGeek collections of **Asianofchaos** (Remy) and **celestinpro** (Jonathan) into one list, with an "Owned by" pill showing Remy, Jonathan or Both.

## Features

- **Cute board-game design:** game cards with BGG box art, owner meeples (Remy, Jonathan, Both), BGG rating, best player count, playing time and weight, with light and dark modes.
- **Welcome card:** a short "something for everyone" intro to the collection, plus a sync status showing when the data was last refreshed from BGG.
- **Nested add-ons:** expansions and accessories sit under the base game they belong to, with their own owner shown when it differs. In the tile view, add-ons BGG links to an edition we don't own are tucked under the edition we do own with the same name; ones with no match get no tile but still show up in search, the table and exports.
- **Search and filters:** search by title, mechanic, category, designer or publisher; filter by BGG's "best with" player count (numbered buttons for every count on the shelf, up to 10+) and mechanics or categories (one combined list, any selected); in the table, those two live in the Best with and Mechanics column filters instead and mechanics (any selected); sort by any column (name, rating, BGG rank, players, best with, time, complexity, year, mechanics, categories or owner). Active filters show as removable chips.
- **Rows view (the default):** a featured banner, then rows of games to scroll sideways, like a streaming app. The banner rotates through our games that are on BGG's hotness list right now, hottest first (topped up with our best-ranked games if few of ours are hot), with a "Surprise me" button. Rows: the Top 10 by BGG rank, then themed rows (on the shelf but not played yet, from Remy's BGG "played" list, shown once a sync has brought that list in; perfect for two, co-ops, quick games, party picks, campaigns, solo, brain burners, long games, fantasy, space, animals, card games, dice, deduction, big groups, newest, oldest). "Deal new rows" swaps in a random set built from the collection: categories, mechanics, designers, publishers, player counts, decades, lengths, weights and mixes of them, different every time. "See all" opens a row as a grid. Filters narrow every row; searching shows a results grid. Opening a game from a row lets you arrow or swipe along that row.
- **Cards or table:** switch between box-art cards and a sortable table (click a column header to sort, Shift-click or use the "Sorted by" bar to add more sort levels, up to four; expand a game's row to see its add-ons). The choice is remembered on the device.
- **Columns:** in the table, show or hide columns and change their order (drag them or use the arrows in the Columns menu; Name always shows). The layout is remembered on the device.
- **Saved views:** in the table, save the current sort order, columns and filters under a name (up to 10), then reapply, rename or delete them from the Saved views menu. They're kept in each visitor's own browser.
- **Column filters:** the table has a filter under every column (Mechanics and Categories open a list of every one on the shelf, with counts: type to find one, and pick several to show games with any of them) (name, rating, BGG rank, players, best with, time, complexity, year, mechanics, categories, owner). They combine with each other and with search, show as chips, and stay applied in card view.
- **Export to Excel:** download the games currently showing (with search, filters and sort applied, across all pages) or the whole collection as an .xlsx file. Add-ons get their own rows, with the game they belong to; the file has a frozen header, filters, BGG links and an About sheet.
- **Installable app (PWA):** the site can be installed to a phone's home screen or as a desktop app (an "Install app" button appears where the browser supports it; on iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen). A service worker keeps the pages and the last collection, so the site opens offline and shows a note while offline. Box art isn't stored offline; games show their drawing instead.
- **Game night planner (`/tools/game-night`):** quick picks for tonight. Pick who's playing (saved profiles, plus guests without one) and how long you have. Each person rates, from 1 ("won't play") to 5 ("love it"), the four complexity levels, 16 mechanic groups and 13 theme groups (defined in `tools/gn-core.js`, each a family of BGG mechanics or categories). A game's fit for a person blends their ratings for its complexity, mechanics and themes; the group fit leans on the least-happy person so picks work for everyone, plus a bump for BGG's best-at-count and rating. Anything containing something a player rated 1 is left out. It suggests three games, explains why, rerolls without repeats, lists everything, and can skip games played in the last 2 weeks, month or 3 months. Profiles from the older version (favorite weights, loves, won't-plays) convert to ratings automatically. "Share these people" shares the profiles by code (`api/group.js`, Vercel Blob).
- **Game nights (`/tools/event`):** plan a night together. The organizer names it, suggests up to 8 times, picks a repeat (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly) and invites people from the planner (their ratings come along), then shares the link or 6-character code. Anyone with it says who they are (or joins with a new name) and: marks each time ✓/?/✕ (the best time is highlighted), rates what they like and sets a time limit, adds games to the vote (from suggestions that fit the head count, time and everyone's ratings, or any game on the shelf) and votes 😍 must play / 👍 happy to / 👎 rather not. Everything updates live on every open device. The organizer (the device that created it, which holds a secret key) can edit details, add or remove times, lock in a time, and build the night: a timeline from arrival through each game (teach & set-up time from the game's weight, play time, short breaks) using the top-voted games that fit, which they can reorder or change. Anyone can download a calendar file once a time is locked. Repeating nights get "Plan the next one", which makes the next night in the series with the same people and ratings and links the two. Game nights you've opened are listed on the page and on the planner.
  - Storage: a Supabase project (`our-game-shelf`, us-east-1) rather than Vercel Blob, whose free limits (about 10,000 reads and 2,000 writes a month, after which Blob switches off for 30 days) live updates would use up. Tables `gn_events`, `gn_players`, `gn_ballot`, `gn_votes` have row-level security on and no policies, so the browser can't touch them directly; it calls `gn_*` Postgres functions (security definer) that need the event code, and the organizer's key for organizer changes. Triggers send a Realtime broadcast on `gn:<CODE>` after every change; pages listen and refetch (falling back to checking every 20 seconds if the live connection drops). Removing a player or a game is a soft delete. The page uses the project's public anon key. Free Supabase projects pause after a week with no activity; opening the Supabase dashboard wakes it.
- **Our notes:** the comment each of us left on a game in our BGG collection shows in that game's details (including notes on its add-ons). Only public notes; BGG's private comments aren't available to the site.
- **Game details:** cover, rating, description, players, best count, playing time, weight, mechanics, categories, designers, publishers and add-ons, with a link to BGG. In card view, use the left and right arrow keys, the arrow buttons beside the details, or swipe to move to the previous or next game (following the current sort and filters, across pages).
- **Charts page (`/stats`):** a dashboard of charts that updates with each sync: games by year, complexity, play time, complexity vs. play time, best player counts, and the top mechanics, categories, designers and publishers. Filters at the top (base games / with expansions / everything, best with, complexity, play time, decade) change every chart. Click any bar, point or dot (or a number in the "Show the numbers" table) to see the games that make it up, with links to BGG. "Our 2026" (for any year, or a quarter of it) recaps our logged plays, from Remy's BGG account: plays, different games, games new to us, the favorite and the next four, plays by month or week, and fun facts compared with the period before. A "Games we've played" section shows Remy's logged plays on BGG by the numbers (plays logged, games played, most played, games on the shelf not played yet) and a times-played chart, top 20 or all; it shows games on our shelf by default and can include games she's played that we don't own. Below that, a chart builder: pick bars, sideways bars, a line or dots, what to group by, what to show (count or average rating, complexity or play time) and what to split or color by, then save charts by name (up to 12, kept in the browser).
- **Pagination:** up to 10 page numbers at a time around the current page; 10, 25, 50, 100, 200 or 300 games per page (remembered on the device); filters stay applied while paging. On tablets and phones the filters open in a drawer.

- **Tools (`/tools`):** a hub of game night helpers grouped into three stops on a game night: Before you play (planner, quiz), At the table (initiative tracker, first player picker) and After the game (a link to the year recap on Charts, which `/stats#recap` scrolls to). Chips at the top jump to each section. Dashed cards are ideas for future tools. Add a tool to the right section in the `SECTIONS` list in `tools/index.html`, swapping out an idea if it was one.
- **Gamer personality quiz (`/tools/quiz`):** ten quick questions score you across six types (Strategist, Tactician, Social Gamer, Explorer, Competitor, Collector) and show your main type, your full mix, and nine games from our shelf that suit that mix. Each type has a list of mechanics, categories, weight and time range it likes (the Social Gamer also likes big player counts, the Competitor games that shine at 2, the Collector games with add-ons on the shelf); a game's score blends those by your mix and the tags show why it matched. You can browse any type's games without taking the quiz. The result is saved in the browser.
- **First player picker (`/tools/first-player`):** everyone puts a finger on the phone; after two seconds of holding still one finger is picked to go first (or every finger gets a turn-order number). Lifting or adding a finger restarts the countdown; lift all fingers to go again. Uses pointer events, so it handles up to 10 touches. "Names" mode (the default on computers) picks from a saved list of names instead. Picks use `crypto.getRandomValues`.
- **Campaign tracker (`/tools/campaigns`):** a generic campaign framework plus game setups. A setup (template, in `tools/campaign-templates.js`) only defines structure, never game content: character fields (numbers, XP with a level hint, rows of boxes like checkmarks, text, notes, resource grids), campaign sections (tracks, resource supply, a week calendar with seasons, buildings with level and condition), achievement lists and kinds of unlocks. Built in: Frosthaven (outpost tracks, supply of the 9 materials and herbs, 80-week summer/winter calendar with notes per week, buildings, campaign stickers, party achievements), Gloomhaven 2nd Edition (prosperity, reputation, donations, global and party achievements), Jaws of the Lion, and a blank one. Players add scenarios as their game opens them, with what each unlocks (by number) and what it needs (achievements that must or must not be in place, plus free text); logging an attempt records date, result, level, who played, achievements gained and anything unlocked, and a win marks it done and opens the scenarios it unlocks. Characters have the setup's fields, can be in the party, benched or retired, and link a personal goal; when the goal is done they're flagged ready to retire, and retiring logs the date and any unlock. Every change goes into a dated history. Setup lets you add/remove fields, tracks, lists and unlock kinds, save the result as your own template, export JSON, and turn on a backup code. Campaigns live in the browser (`game-shelf:campaigns`); the backup code keeps a copy in Supabase (`shelf_backups`, via `backup_put`/`backup_get`, `tools/shelf-sync.js`) that another device can open, and changes save to it automatically.
- **Personal goals (`/tools/goals`):** goals made of steps (checkboxes, or counts to reach). A goal can be linked to a character in a campaign on this device; the campaign shows its progress (and keeps a snapshot so it travels with the campaign's backup) and flags the character ready to retire when it's complete. Goals live in the browser (`game-shelf:goals`) with an optional backup code.
- **Initiative tracker (`/tools/initiative`):** add combatants, then roll a random turn order or type in initiative numbers (highest or lowest first, with arrows to settle ties), and step through turns in a full list or one at a time, round after round. Keep several parties and switch between them. Star (★) the core party members; everyone else is scenario-only (enemies and the like), shown in a "This scenario" list that one button clears when the scenario ends, keeping the party. Core status is just a toggle, so retired characters can be unstarred or removed and new ones starred. "Share" gives a party a 6-character code and a link; anyone who opens it gets the party on their device, and adding or removing names syncs between everyone using that code. Initiative numbers and turns stay on each device.

## How it works

- `index.html`: the games page.
- `tools/index.html`: the Tools page.
- `tools/game-night.html`: the game night planner.
- `tools/quiz.html`: the gamer personality quiz.
- `tools/first-player.html`: the first player picker.
- `tools/campaigns.html`, `tools/campaign-templates.js`: the campaign tracker and its setups.
- `tools/goals.html`: personal goals.
- `tools/shelf-sync.js`: backup codes (Supabase) for campaigns and goals.
- `tools/event.html`: game nights (events, availability, ratings, votes, schedule).
- `tools/gn-core.js`: shared taste ratings, scoring and the ratings editor for the planner and game nights.
- `tools/initiative.html`: the initiative tracker (served at `/tools/initiative`; the old `/initiative` address redirects there, query string included).
- `api/group.js`: shared game night groups (people and preferences), using the same store.
- `api/plays.js`: serves Remy's individual logged plays (saved by the daily sync to `shelf/plays.json`) for the "Our year" recaps.
- `api/party.js`: a small code-based store (`codeStore`); saves and loads shared initiative parties in Vercel Blob (`parties/<CODE>.json`).
- `stats.html`: the charts page (served at `/stats`; uses Chart.js 4.4.1 from cdnjs).
- `api/sync.js`: the daily sync. Vercel Cron runs it at 10:00 UTC (see `vercel.json`). It fetches both owned collections from the BGG XML API2, fetches Remy's logged plays (every play, page by page), BGG's hotness list, our collection notes and Remy's play counts (including games she's played but we don't own), refreshes game details (images, ratings, overall rank, weight, playing time, mechanics, categories, designers, publishers, descriptions, player counts, BGG's best-player-count summary and links between games) that are missing or more than six days old, in batches of 20 with a pause between requests, and saves a snapshot to Vercel Blob. If BGG is unavailable, the previous snapshot stays up and the page notes that the day's refresh failed.
- `api/collection.js`: serves the saved snapshot to the page. It never calls BGG, so it's fast no matter how many people visit.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `pwa.js`, `icons/`: the installable app: manifest, offline service worker (bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` to clear old caches), install button and offline note, and the app icons.
- `lib/bgg.js`: BGG fetching and parsing.

BGG data is shown as BGG provides it.

## Setup

Environment variables in Vercel:

- `BGG_API_TOKEN`: a BGG application token (register at https://boardgamegeek.com/applications).
- `CRON_SECRET`: authorizes the sync; Vercel Cron sends it automatically. To run a sync by hand, open `/api/sync?secret=<CRON_SECRET>`.
- `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`: added by connecting the project's Blob store.

Pushes to `main` deploy automatically.
